Raised in Viçosa

Born in Ipatinga, Brazil, in 1987, Guilherme Pereira de Carvalho holds a degree in Computer Science from the Federal University of Viçosa. He spent a mere 9 years there, and made the most of every one of them.

Family and home

He is Joice's husband and Elisa's dad. From Minas Gerais at heart, he lives there for now, but he has also lived in Joinville and Navegantes, in Santa Catarina, and will go back someday: he loves both places. Along the way he also lived in Belo Horizonte.

Work

A front-end engineer, he has worked for an American company for more than 5 years, with React, TypeScript and Ruby on Rails. He is passionate about front-end development, especially component libraries and code organization.

Before that, he volunteered for years at a student-run consulting company and co-founded a company where he worked for three years. In his spare time he uses his creativity to build his own apps (take a look at them).

Fixes and inventions

He is well known for his gambiarras, the Brazilian word for clever improvised fixes, and for repairing just about anything: if it broke, he'll find a way. He loves electronics and making new things, from the soldering iron to the code editor.

A year in Dublin

He went on an exchange to Dublin, Ireland: he worked, studied English and travelled a lot. There he had some of the most valuable experiences of his life, meeting one-of-a-kind people and fabulous places.

Music

He can't live without his daily dose of music, mostly good old rock, but old-school Brazilian country, some MPB and a good forró to dance to have their place too. He plays the drums for fun, plays the guitar for his daughter and takes forró dance classes with Joice.

Weekend athlete

He swims, has run a half marathon and has a yellow belt in judo. He has a dormant passion for mountain biking and is now learning to surf.

Who he is

Positive, cheerful and playful, he can make lifelong friends in a few minutes. Still, he's objective, precise and direct when he needs to be.
