From Ipatinga to Viçosa

Born in Ipatinga, Brazil, in 1987, Guilherme Pereira de Carvalho holds a degree in Computer Science from the Federal University of Viçosa and lived in Viçosa, Minas Gerais, for a mere 9 very well spent years.

Work

He loves his work and has a strong entrepreneurial streak. He volunteered for years at a student-run consulting company, co-founded a company where he worked for three years, and today runs a startup that is a dream come true.

A year in Dublin

He lived in Dublin, Ireland, for a year. There he had some of the most valuable experiences of his life, meeting one-of-a-kind people and fabulous places.

Music

He simply can't live without his daily dose of music. He's mostly into good rock, but thinks old-school Brazilian country and a good forró to dance to have their place too.

Weekend athlete

Crazy about cycling and enjoys running too, though he's still learning to take good care of his knees.

Who he is

A fan of personal development and coaching books. He believes every human being has infinite potential to do amazing things, but sadly not everyone is aware of it yet.

Positive, cheerful and playful, he can make lifelong friends in a few minutes. Still, he's objective, precise and direct when he needs to be.
